A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you press play.

Does MiruShin include films or shows?+

MiruShin is a media player and discovery interface. It does not host or provide media, and does not ship Sora modules. Playback depends on sources you configure and have the right to use.

How do I add sources?+

Open Addons and paste a trusted module manifest URL, then install and enable the module. See the Sora community for information about Sora-compatible addons. Third-party modules are maintained by their own authors.

Do I need an account?+

There is no MiruShin account. Discovery works without one. Connect AniList, MyAnimeList, or Shikimori for tracking; AniList also provides its profile and social features. Google Drive Sync is optional.

What travels between my devices?+

Optional Drive Sync restores library checkpoints, playback and app preferences, addons, API connections, and tracker sessions using your Google account's private app data. Google OAuth tokens and temporary watch-party credentials stay out of synced records. Read the Privacy Policy for the full details.

Can I use it on my TV?+

The Android APK supports Android TV, with layouts and navigation designed for a remote. The iOS build is an IPA for iPhone and iPad.

Where can I report a problem?+

Use GitHub Issues with your app version, platform, and steps to reproduce it. For playback pauses, enable Playback diagnostics in the app's Player Settings and save a report manually.
