Optional Cloud Sync and Account Credentials

Google Drive Sync is optional. When enabled, MiruShin may store complete Local Library checkpoints, cached presentation metadata and artwork URLs, playback data, addons, tracker account profiles and sessions, user-provided API credentials, persistent relay preferences, and Library/app settings in the private appDataFolder of the Google account selected by the user. Each AniList account uses an isolated library workspace, its own MAL/Shikimori connections, and its own Library preferences.

You are responsible for choosing and securing the Google account and devices used for synchronization. Restored third-party sessions remain subject to provider terms and may expire, be revoked, or stop working. MiruShin does not guarantee uninterrupted sync or continued credential validity. Google Drive OAuth credentials, MiruShin-operated OAuth client secrets, and temporary Watch with Friends room credentials are not included in synced records.

Disconnect only stops Drive Sync on that device. The separate Delete account action removes MiruShin-owned records from the selected account's private appDataFolder and disconnects the device without deleting its local library or the user's Google account.
