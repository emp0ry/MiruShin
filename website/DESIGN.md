# MiruShin · EMP0RY product site

23 October 2026. Product supplement to [EMP0RY v19](design/EMP0RY.md).

The shared foundation is graphite `#09090d`, self-hosted Geist, precise spacing, restrained lavender interaction and visible focus. Use a 1320px content maximum, fluid 20–72px gutters and comfortable 44px controls. Keep EMP0RY's lowercase 900 wordmark, with a quiet product breadcrumb. Navigation is a fixed sibling of one clipped native scrollport, preserving the homepage's Safari containment fix.

MiruShin's signature is its 新 mark: stable point samples of the existing authored contours, gently breathing over a 24-second loop. Assemble once, react locally to the pointer and overlapping clicks, return to the same recognizable mark. Preserve a static SVG fallback, keyboard activation, reduced-motion pose and offscreen/hidden pause. No simulated playback or fake app settings. The actual product is shown through its own screenshots.

Lead with a clear promise, a real download destination and a focused mathematical mark. Follow with an editorial screenshot stage, the library/sync story, playback and Watch with Friends, platform downloads, getting started and concise answers. Vary composition, not the foundation. Scale the hero from approximately 33px on a 320px phone through 41px at 390px to an 84px desktop cap; use 31–48px section headings, 16–18px introductory copy and 13–16px body. Keep supporting labels subordinate. Do not stack an enormous category title above an enormous product name, insert empty viewport panels, repeat feature cards, or add noisy technical labels.

Buttons match the homepage’s Explore control: translucent graphite, a thin lavender border, 5px corners and a light arrow; never use a solid lavender CTA fill. The footer’s decorative mark is muted. End with FAQ and the footer; do not repeat a bottom download pitch.

The screenshot viewer is a presentation control, not a demo of the app. Show Discovery and Player at their full original aspect ratio on every breakpoint; never crop their bottom controls. Use authentic local board/player/mobile captures, optimized as WebP, with a full-size accessible viewer and captions. Discovery, Player and On mobile share the same responsive stage height; shrink desktop captures with contain rather than cropping. Keep the Take a closer look link. The small art signature contains only MIRUSHIN / A NEW WAY TO WATCH, without a repeated 新 glyph. Keep third-party imagery attribution. The download selector shows actual release formats and requests the current official GitHub release once. Verify download URLs against the expected owner/repository; missing or failed assets fall back to the official release page. Every destination remains in static HTML without JavaScript. Never claim a build, minimum OS, signature or store listing that has not been verified.

Privacy/legal wording and dates are retained from existing published HTML. Reskin those pages with the same header, font and reading layout, using a compact table of contents. Preserve `source.json`, the app-link bridge, custom domain, compatibility anchors, original icon URLs and source automation. Keep the existing GA4 property, host-gated so local traffic is excluded. Production metadata and the social preview must match the new product identity.

Responsive behavior: natural content heights; compact mobile menu with Escape/focus restoration; keyboard-navigable tabs for screenshot and OS selection; native details for FAQs. Compact controls keep at least a 44px hit area. Long sticky policy contents scroll independently on short screens and join the natural document flow on phones. Make font/image/graphics/network failure states useful. No hidden-content entrance gate, global cursor replacement, autoplay video, external font request or unrelated deployment service.
